Help & campaign information

Frequently Asked Questions

The campaign answers below are adapted from the Nusuk app’s Help → Support Center section. The unofficial-project notice comes first for clarity.

Is this an official Nusuk website?

No. This is an unofficial, open-source community project created by Zake. It is not affiliated with Nusuk or the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. The Nusuk app and its official terms always take precedence.

How do I get keys?

Nusuk provides one free key each day, and it expires at the end of that day. A successful game share also gives one key to you and one to the person who receives the share.

How many keys can I use per day?

You can use one key per day to enter the challenge.

When is the draw held?

A random draw is held once a day. Winners are announced through Nusuk’s official @MyNusuk accounts.

What happens if I answer after the draw?

A correct answer submitted after a draw is not included in that draw; it moves automatically to the next draw shown in the app.

Who is eligible to participate?

Participants must be at least 18. All nationalities may participate unless local law prohibits it. Participation also means accepting the official terms and complying with applicable laws.

What are the prizes?

First prize: an Umrah trip for the winner and one chosen companion, including the Umrah visa, travel tickets, ten nights in Makkah and Madinah, transportation, and guidance under the official conditions. It cannot be transferred, exchanged, or redeemed for cash.

Second prize: 20 cartons of Zamzam water. The winner may gift them, but delivery is limited to citizens and residents inside Saudi Arabia under the official conditions.

Can I choose an Umrah companion?

Yes. The Umrah winner may choose one companion, subject to the official competition terms.

How will a winner be contacted?

Winners need a valid email address for the notification and prize coordination. If a winner cannot be reached within 30 days, the right to the prize is lost.

Can the prize be transferred to someone else?

The Umrah prize is non-transferable and belongs only to the officially announced winner.

Where can I see previous winners?

Use the Winners page in the Nusuk game, check the official @MyNusuk social accounts, or use this site’s Official Winners directory as a community index.

How is competition data handled?

According to Nusuk Help, participant data is used for the competition, the draw, and contacting winners under the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah’s approved privacy policy. This unofficial site does not request or store your personal details.

Is participation free?

Yes. Participation in the competition is free.

What should I do if the Nusuk app has a technical problem?

For sign-in problems, error messages, or other app issues, contact official Nusuk technical support at 920002814.

Can the same person win more than once?

No. A person may win only once, and their draw entry closes after the win is recorded.

Can the organizer change or cancel the competition or prizes?

Yes. The organizer may make changes or cancel the competition or prizes when emergency or regulatory circumstances require it.

How are prize confirmation and trip dates arranged?

The relevant team contacts the winner to confirm the prize and arrange the trip date based on availability and the approved terms.

Can a prize be shared with family?

It depends on the prize. The Umrah trip is for the winner and one chosen companion and cannot be transferred. Zamzam water may be gifted, provided delivery remains within Saudi Arabia to eligible citizens or residents.
